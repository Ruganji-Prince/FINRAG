# Corpus
Only official public publications belong in the corpus. `manifest.json` is curated source metadata.
Publication dates are taken from the report; month-only dates use day 01 and are explicitly described in usage_notes.
Raw downloaded PDFs and local databases are ignored by Git. Do not add private documents.
NISR GDP 2025 explicitly states CC BY 4.0 on PDF page 2. Preserve attribution and source links.
Other reports require per-document rights verification before inclusion or redistribution.
Test fixtures are generated independently and never loaded into the production corpus.
